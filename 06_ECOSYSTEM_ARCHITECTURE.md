# 06_ECOSYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE

Status: Strategic Development Document

Purpose:

This document describes the possible long-term evolution of the Führen & Folgen methodology.

It is intentionally broader than a workshop, product, or event.

It describes an ecosystem.

---

# Core Assumption

The methodology is the primary asset.

The workshop is one delivery format.

The long-term value resides in:

- principles
- observation architecture
- facilitation capability
- transfer evidence
- community knowledge

---

# Ecosystem Layers

Layer 1
Method

Layer 2
Workshop

Layer 3
Alumni Community

Layer 4
Facilitator Development

Layer 5
Research & Evidence

Layer 6
Institutional Partnerships

Layer 7
Licensing

These layers build on one another.

---

# Year 1

Primary objectives:

- clarify language
- refine Method Canon
- run workshops
- improve observables
- gather participant feedback
- document transfer

Success metric:

Method becomes clearer.

---

# Year 2

Potential developments:

- advanced workshops
- alumni practice groups
- facilitator apprenticeships
- observation training
- case documentation

Success metric:

Method becomes teachable.

---

# Year 3

Potential developments:

- facilitator certification
- train-the-trainer systems
- licensed facilitator pairs
- institutional delivery

Success metric:

Method becomes transmissible.

---

# Alumni Community

Participants are more than customers.

They become:

- practitioners
- observers
- contributors
- evidence generators

The alumni layer may eventually function as a community of practice.

---

# Facilitator Pathway

Possible pathway:

Participant
→ Advanced Participant
→ Assistant Facilitator
→ Facilitator
→ Senior Facilitator

Each stage requires increasing observation capacity.

---

# Research Layer

Research questions include:

- What transfers?
- Under which conditions?
- What remains stable?
- Which interventions create change?
- Which observations are most reliable?

The ecosystem should learn from itself.

---

# Institutional Opportunities

Possible future environments:

- conservatories
- orchestras
- universities
- leadership programs
- health professions
- educational institutions

Selection should follow fit rather than scale.

---

# Licensing

Licensing should never precede clarity.

Only after:

- method definition
- facilitator standards
- evidence accumulation
- quality assurance

does licensing become relevant.

---

# What Should Not Scale

The ecosystem should protect:

- observation quality
- depth
- integrity
- participant experience

Growth should never destroy the qualities that created value.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The ultimate goal is not expansion.

The ultimate goal is preserving and transmitting a useful methodology without dilution.
