# 03_MARKET_LANGUAGE

Status: Communication Source of Truth

Purpose:

This document records the language used to describe the work,
the problems it addresses, and the conversations it creates.

It does not define the method.

It defines how people find the method.

---

# Strategic Observation

People rarely buy a workshop.

They buy movement toward something they value.

Or relief from something they experience.

Therefore communication starts with lived reality.

Not methodology.

---

# The Problem With Technical Language

Most people do not search for:

- human navigation
- embodied process
- resonance
- self-contact

Even if these are central to the work.

Communication must therefore bridge experience and method.

---

# Experience Language

People often describe:

- feeling stuck
- losing access under pressure
- overthinking
- difficulties leading
- difficulties following
- communication breakdown
- uncertainty in groups
- conflict avoidance
- excessive control
- lack of trust
- hesitation

This is where conversations begin.

---

# Method Language

The method describes these same phenomena as:

- human navigation
- relationship dynamics
- embodied process
- coordination
- resonance
- self-contact
- timing
- adaptability

The role of communication is translation.

---

# The Translation Principle

Never dilute the method.

Never begin with the method.

Start where the participant lives.

Guide toward deeper distinctions.

---

# Human Navigation

One of the strongest framing concepts.

Definition:

The ability to orient and act effectively in relationship under changing conditions.

Applications:

- leadership
- teamwork
- education
- performance
- health professions
- family systems
- coaching

Human navigation may eventually become a primary umbrella term.

---

# Relationship

Relationship is not a feeling.

Relationship is an ongoing process.

This distinction consistently creates interest.

Potential framing:

"Most professional challenges are relationship challenges disguised as technical problems."

---

# Embodied Process

Embodied process explains why insight alone is insufficient.

People often understand.

They do not reliably act.

The work addresses that gap.

---

# The Workshop Problem

A strategic insight from project development:

The workshop itself may be too small a frame.

The workshop is a vehicle.

The deeper asset is the methodology.

Future communication should increasingly point beyond a single event.

---

# Emerging Ecosystem Language

Potential future terms:

- Method
- Practice
- System
- Facilitator Pathway
- Professional Development
- Community of Practice
- Certification
- Licensing
- Train the Trainer

These concepts belong to future architecture, but should be tracked here because they influence language choices today.

---

# Language To Avoid

Avoid:

- transformation promises
- motivational hype
- personal development clichés
- guru language
- breakthrough marketing
- exaggerated claims

The work gains credibility through precision.

---

# Tone

Calm.

Adult.

Precise.

Observational.

Curious.

Non-defensive.

Depth without drama.

---

# Core Positioning Hypothesis

The project is not fundamentally about leading and following.

Leading and following are the laboratory.

The deeper subject is human navigation in relationship.
