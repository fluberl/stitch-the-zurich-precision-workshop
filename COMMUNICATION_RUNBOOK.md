# Participant Communication Runbook

Use this runbook for calm, precise participant communication in contact/application workflows.

## Operating Rules

- Keep messages short and specific.
- Confirm receipt and next steps clearly.
- Avoid sales pressure, urgency, or motivational language.
- Use plain operational wording and realistic timelines.

## SLA Targets

- Contact response: within 1-2 business days
- Application acknowledgment: immediate automated confirmation
- Application review update: within 3 business days
- Post-conversation decision: within 2 business days

## Manual vs Automated

### Manual (required)
- Fit/alignment judgments
- Acceptance vs waitlist decisions
- Sensitive participant questions
- Exception handling and special accommodations

### Automated (safe)
- Contact confirmation
- Application confirmation
- Internal submission alert
- Status-change transactional emails
- Fixed pre-workshop reminders

## Transactional Templates (Approved Set)

## 1) Contact confirmation
Subject: `We received your message`

Body:
```
Hello {first_name},

Thank you for your message.
We have received your inquiry and will respond within 1-2 business days.

Best regards,
Leading & Following Workshop Zurich
```

## 2) Application confirmation
Subject: `Application received`

Body:
```
Hello {first_name},

Thank you for your application.
We review each application manually and will send a first update within 3 business days.

Best regards,
Leading & Following Workshop Zurich
```

## 3) Accepted
Subject: `Application update: accepted`

Body:
```
Hello {first_name},

Thank you for your application.
We are pleased to confirm your acceptance for {workshop_cohort}.

Next step:
- We will clarify any remaining practical questions.
- You will then receive confirmation and preparation details.

Best regards,
Leading & Following Workshop Zurich
```

## 4) Waitlist
Subject: `Application update: waitlist`

Body:
```
Hello {first_name},

Thank you for your application.
At the moment, this cohort is full, and we have placed your application on the waitlist.

If a place opens, we will contact you directly.

Best regards,
Leading & Following Workshop Zurich
```

## 5) Confirmed + preparation
Subject: `Participation confirmed: next preparation steps`

Body:
```
Hello {first_name},

Your participation for {workshop_cohort} is confirmed.

Preparation details:
- schedule and arrival window
- what to bring
- practical venue information
- final contact point for questions

Best regards,
Leading & Following Workshop Zurich
```

## Internal Notification Format

For contact/application alerts include:
- full name
- email
- form type (contact/application)
- submission timestamp
- CRM contact link
- short extracted summary (first 300 chars)

## Broadcast Policy

Allowed:
- logistics updates for confirmed cohort
- preparation reminders (D-14, D-3)
- brief post-workshop follow-up

Not allowed:
- persuasion or nurture campaigns
- frequent promotional sequences
- automated behavior-based branching
