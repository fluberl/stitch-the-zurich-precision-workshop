# 02_WORKSHOP_MANIFESTO

Status: Foundational Document

The workshop is not the method.

The workshop is the environment in which the method becomes visible.

---

# Why This Exists

Many people spend years acquiring knowledge, techniques, qualifications, and experience.

Yet under pressure something important disappears.

Timing degrades.

Perception narrows.

Action becomes effortful.

Relationship becomes management.

The workshop exists to investigate this phenomenon directly.

---

# The Core Observation

Human beings do not act in isolation.

They act in relationship.

Leadership.
Following.
Coordination.
Negotiation.
Trust.
Timing.

All emerge in relationship.

The workshop uses simple practical tasks to make these dynamics visible.

---

# What We Are Actually Exploring

Not dance.

Not therapy.

Not performance.

Not role play.

Not communication theory.

We explore:

- contact
- timing
- attention
- coordination
- uncertainty
- adaptation
- self-contact
- human navigation

---

# The Central Question

What happens between leading and following?

The visible action is rarely the interesting part.

The interesting part is the process that produces it.

---

# The Six Dynamics

Ohne Folgen kein Führen.

Ohne Wahrnehmung keine Führung.

Ohne Resonanz keine Koordination.

Ohne Loslassen keine Zusammenarbeit.

Ohne Eigenkontakt keine Präsenz.

Ohne Unsicherheit keine echte Begegnung.

These are not slogans.

They are working hypotheses tested in practice.

---

# Why Movement

Movement creates visibility.

Thoughts can be hidden.

Movement cannot.

The body continuously reveals:

- hesitation
- timing
- effort
- adaptation
- rigidity
- responsiveness

Movement therefore becomes a practical laboratory.

---

# Small Groups

The workshop is intentionally small.

Observation quality matters more than scale.

Participants are seen.

Interactions are observed.

Feedback remains specific.

The workshop favors depth over throughput.

---

# Dual Facilitation

Two complementary perspectives remain active throughout.

One facilitator tracks:

- access
- regulation
- pressure
- self-contact

The other tracks:

- timing
- interaction
- resonance
- coordination

Together they create a richer observation field.

---

# The Desired Outcome

Participants leave with:

- greater awareness
- improved timing
- increased adaptability
- greater tolerance for uncertainty
- clearer perception of relationship dynamics
- improved transfer into daily life and work

---

# What Success Looks Like

Success is not a powerful workshop experience.

Success is transfer.

The question is always:

What became possible afterwards?

---

# Tone

Calm.

Precise.

Adult.

Non-promotional.

Deep rather than dramatic.

The work does not depend on belief.

It depends on observation.
