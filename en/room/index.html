The Workshop Room

The work takes place in a small group in the Zurich area.

The room offers enough space for movement, partner work, and shared tasks. It is simple, bright, and quiet enough for attention to remain with what is happening in the moment: orientation, coordination, and contact.

The exact location will be shared with confirmed participants in good time.

Why this room?

  • enough space for practical work in movement
  • calm atmosphere without unnecessary distraction
  • suitable for individual work, partner work, and group tasks
  • easy to reach in the Zurich area
