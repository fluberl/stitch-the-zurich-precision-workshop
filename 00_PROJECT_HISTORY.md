# Phase 0 — Origins

Before the website existed, the project lived primarily as:

- conversations between Vincent and Ulf
- workshop design discussions
- the 78-page strategic workshop document
- workshop flyers
- experiential teaching practice

The central question was already present:

> What happens between leading and following?

---

# Phase 1 — Workshop Framing

The project initially appeared as:

"Leading & Following — Two Sides of the Same Coin"

Primary focus:

- workshop design
- audience definition
- pricing
- positioning
- communication

Key discoveries:

- professionals often know what to do but lose access under pressure
- leading and following are relational rather than hierarchical
- reliability matters more than knowledge

---

# Phase 2 — Website Development

A workshop website was developed.

Important shift:

The website became more than an event page.

A distinction emerged between:

- workshop
- methodology
- intellectual property

Key observation:

"Current content is excellent landing copy, but not yet a full methodology publication."

This became a foundational strategic turning point.

---

# Phase 3 — Method Canon Emerges

On 29 May / 1 June 2026 the Method Canon emerged as a separate internal document.

Key decision:

> Site copy should align over time, not lead the canon.

Consequences:

- method precedes marketing
- workshop becomes delivery vehicle
- website becomes publication layer

---

# Phase 4 — Human Navigation

The project shifted from:

- performance optimisation
- self-regulation under pressure

toward:

- human navigation
- relationship as embodied process
- practical exploration

New concepts appeared:

- Human Navigation
- Beziehung
- Embodied Process
- Das Selbstverständliche
- Grundlegende Dynamik

---

# Phase 5 — Future Architecture

Not yet fully developed.

Emerging directions:

- facilitator system
- dual observation protocol
- alumni research community
- evidence architecture
- licensing
- certification
- conservatory and institutional partnerships

These remain future-development areas.

# Historical Principle

The workshop is important.

The website is important.

The Method Canon is more fundamental.

The long-term asset is the evolving practice system that makes human navigation observable, teachable, and transferable.
