# 07_RESEARCH_ARCHIVE

Status: Living Research Document

Purpose:

This document collects observations, evidence, transfer examples,
questions, hypotheses, and discoveries emerging from practice.

Unlike the Method Canon, this document is expected to evolve continuously.

---

# Core Principle

The methodology should learn from its own application.

Research is not separate from practice.

Research emerges through careful observation.

---

# What Belongs Here

Examples:

- workshop observations
- participant reports
- transfer stories
- facilitator observations
- recurring patterns
- failed assumptions
- emerging hypotheses
- institutional experiences

---

# Evidence Philosophy

The project values:

- observation
- repeatability
- transfer
- practical usefulness

Evidence may be:

- quantitative
- qualitative
- narrative
- observational

No single evidence type is sufficient.

# Emerging Hypotheses

Examples:

- Coordination improves when perception improves.
- Tolerance for uncertainty increases adaptability.
- Relationship quality influences performance.
- Observation often precedes change.

Hypotheses remain provisional until repeatedly observed.
