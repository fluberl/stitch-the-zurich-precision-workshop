# 01_METHOD_CANON

Status: Canonical Source of Truth

Purpose:
This document defines the Führen & Folgen practice system.
It describes the methodology itself, not a workshop, website, marketing campaign, or delivery format.

---

# Epistemic Stance

The method treats human navigation in relationship as practically explorable through embodied process and movement.

The aim is not to produce performance, spectacle, inspiration, or belief.

The aim is to make visible what is already operative:

Das Selbstverständliche.

Depth emerges through precision, perception, repetition, and transfer.

---

# Problem

Many professionals and performers know what to do.

Under pressure they lose access.

The failure is not primarily cognitive.

It is relational and embodied.

Manifestations include:

- loss of access
- hesitation
- over-control
- coordination breakdown
- loss of self-contact
- pseudo-certainty

The method addresses reliability of navigation in relationship under pressure.

---

# Core Principles

1. Action over abstraction.
2. Discovery over invention.
3. Embodied process.
4. Immediate feedback.
5. Repeat until observable.
6. Progressive demand.
7. Dual observation.
8. Precision over intensity.
9. Explicit boundaries.
10. Transfer as criterion.

# Grundlegende Dynamik

Ohne Folgen kein Führen.

Ohne Eigenkontakt keine Präsenz.

---

# Core Distinctions

Human Navigation
Orientation in relationship under changing conditions.

Beziehung
Relationship understood as an ongoing embodied process.

Embodied Process
Perception, regulation and action understood as one stream.

Movement
Diagnostic instrument rather than aesthetic product.

Resonance
Mutual readability in contact.

Eigenkontakt
Self-contact while remaining in relationship.

Präsenz
Functional availability to perceive and respond.

Transfer
Observable reappearance in real life.

---

# Observables

Observable shifts include:

- clearer timing
- reduced unnecessary effort
- improved coordination
- faster recovery after perturbation
- improved readability in interaction
- greater tolerance for uncertainty
- improved transfer into professional situations

---

# Boundaries

This method is:

- practical
- relational
- embodied
- feedback-rich

This method is not:

- therapy
- charisma training
- inspiration
- motivational speaking
- wellness branding
- professional skill substitution

---

# Learning Progression

Phase A
Perception and self-contact.

Phase B
Resonance and role navigation.

Phase C
Coordination under increasing demand.

Phase D
Transfer into professional life.

---

# Co-Facilitation Invariant

The methodology assumes two complementary observation channels.

Internal Axis:
- regulation
- access
- pressure
- self-contact

External Axis:
- timing
- interaction
- resonance
- coordination

Together they create a dual observation architecture.

---

# Transfer

The central question is:

What becomes different outside the workshop?

Value is judged by transfer into professional life rather than in-room experience.
